Shellport is a native Linux desktop app that brings together everything you need to work with servers: **SSH terminals**, a **dual-pane SFTP file manager**, **Docker management** (local or over SSH) and an **encrypted secrets vault**, all in one fast window.

It's free, open source (MIT) and private by design: no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.

## What you can do

- Open tabbed **SSH and local terminals** with search, themes and one-click reconnect.
- Save hosts in **colour-coded groups** and sign in with a password, a vault key, a key file or ssh-agent.
- Reach private machines through **jump hosts** and forward ports with **`ssh -L`** tunnels.
- **Upload and download** whole folders with progress and cancel, or drag files in from your desktop.
- Manage **Docker** containers, images, volumes and networks, locally or on any SSH host.
- Keep **passwords, private keys and API tokens** in an encrypted vault.

## Where to go next

1. [Install Shellport](/docs/install/) using the `.deb` package or the AppImage.
2. Follow the [Quick start](/docs/getting-started/) to create your vault and connect to your first server.
3. Browse the feature guides in the sidebar when you need details.
