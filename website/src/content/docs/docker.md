Click the **cube** icon on the left, then pick **Local** or any saved SSH host.

## Local or over SSH

Shellport manages the **local Docker engine** or **any SSH host**. Remote engines are reached through your SSH connection, so no Docker port needs to be exposed.

## Containers

Containers are **grouped by Compose project**. You can start, stop, restart, pause, kill and remove them, or open a shell inside a container.

## Live logs

Follow logs for one container or for **all containers of a project, merged and colour-coded**. Merged logs open in time order. Use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> to search.

## Images, volumes and networks

- Pull and remove images, including from **private registries**.
- Inspect and remove volumes and networks.

## Disk usage and cleanup

The **System** tab shows disk usage and lets you prune unused data.
